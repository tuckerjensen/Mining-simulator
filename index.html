Model boundaries

Closed truck fleet; continuous-time loading, travel, queueing and dumping events. Source availability increases expected loading duration. Truck availability creates occasional delays. Destination throughput caps enforce minimum intervals between completed dumps. Material split targets and minimum rates are soft dispatch priorities, while route eligibility and destination capacity are hard constraints. Scheduled closure windows and shift reports supported. No shared road congestion, physical hopper buffers or full fleet maintenance schedule. Validate with production logs before operational decisions.
